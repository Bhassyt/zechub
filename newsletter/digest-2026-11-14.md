# Zcash Updates ⓩ

## Valar Group, Project Tachyon, Shielded Labs, ZODL & Zcash Foundation Updates

### Valar Group
- **Proposal to Reduce Zcash Block Time from 25 to 20 Seconds:** ValarDragon and Evan Forbes proposed reducing Zcash's target block interval from 25 to 20 seconds to improve payment confirmation speed. The proposal is intended for community discussion and possible inclusion in NU7.1. It has not yet been approved. [Read more](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/proposal-lower-zcash-block-target-spacing-from-25s-to-20s/59035)

### Shielded Labs
- **Network Sustainability Mechanism Reserve Tracker:** Shielded Labs shared an explorer that allows the community to monitor transaction fees accumulating in the reserve following NU7 activation. [Read the post](https://x.com/ShieldedLabs/status/2119784577237488038)

### ZODL
- **ZODL Launches Privacy-Protecting Wallet Servers:** ZODL is rolling out new wallet infrastructure using Oblivious HTTP (OHTTP), with Cloudflare and Fastly relays. This separates users' IP addresses from their wallet requests, making it harder to link wallet activity to a user's IP address. The rollout begins gradually across Android and iOS. [Read more](https://zodl.com/zodl-is-launching-new-wallet-servers-that-protect-your-privacy/)

### Zcash Foundation
- **Zebra Ándale: The Fastest Zebra Yet:** The Zcash Foundation introduced Zebra 7.1.0-alpha.0, a faster version of its Zcash node software. The release improves mining performance, block acceptance, network relay, and mining-pool support. Miners and node operators are invited to test it and provide feedback. [Read more](https://zfnd.org/zebra-andale-the-fastest-zebra-yet/)

## Zcash Community Grants  🏛️

- **Q4 2026 Coinholder-Directed Retroactive Grant Program:** The Q4 round is accepting proposals for completed Zcash ecosystem work. The submission deadline is November 13 at 20:00 UTC. [Read more](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/q4-2026-coinholder-directed-retroactive-grant-program-call-for-proposals/58598)

- **ZCG and the Dev Fund: Timeline for Proposals and Polling:** Five Zcash ecosystem organizations outlined a timeline for community discussion and polling on the future of Zcash Community Grants and the Development Fund. Community feedback on the proposed poll questions runs from November 9 to 15. [Read more](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zcg-and-the-dev-fund-timeline-for-proposals-and-polling/58906)

- **Zafe – Q4 Retroactive Grant Application:** Anmol Bansal submitted a $45,000 retroactive grant application for Zafe, an open-source Android wallet that allows groups to manage and approve shared shielded ZEC payments using FROST multisignature technology. The wallet is currently in beta and has not undergone an external audit. [Read application](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/retroactive-grant-application-zafe/59126)

- **PulseHawk – Independent NU7 Block Timing Measurement:** PulseHawk submitted a $4,500 retroactive grant application for its work measuring Zcash NU7 testnet and mainnet block timing. The application includes block counts, interval data, activation forecasts and an upgrade report with raw data and scripts. [Read application](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/retroactive-grant-application-independent-nu7-block-timing-measurement-pulsehawk/59138)

## Community Projects  🌐

- **Zingo Wallet:** A community discussion highlighted Zingo's user experience and features, including Nym integration, NU7 and Ironwood compatibility, rapid syncing, Zcash names, and swaps. [Read more](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zingo-2026-2028/59043)

- **PetraPay — Shielded ZEC Payment Gateway:** PetraPay is a payment gateway that allows businesses and online stores to accept more than 190 tokens while receiving payments in shielded ZEC. It also supports payment links, invoices, and developer integrations. [Read more](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/petrapay-stripe-style-checkout-that-settles-in-shielded-zec/59022)

- **Zcash East Africa – Developer Meet-up Reminder:** The community shared a reminder about two upcoming opportunities to connect and learn: an X Space at 8:00 PM EAT and an online developer meet-up on Google Meet at 11:00 AM EAT.
